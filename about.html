/The organization

ONE NAME.

SOVEN is an emerging esports and creator organization built around the people willing to put in the work.

What we're building

A competitive presence.
A creative identity.

Our focus is on two connected sides of gaming: competitive players pursuing their next level, and content creators developing their voice and audience.

We're working to bring those ambitions together under a recognizable SOVEN identity. That means taking competition seriously, giving creativity room to grow, and being intentional about how we represent the organization.

We're early in that journey. Our direction is clear: build with people who care about their craft and what they can contribute to a team.
